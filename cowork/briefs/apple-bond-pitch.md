# First Cowork rehearsal — Apple debt capital markets

Use the Banking Studio plugin and combine its IBD writing, public financial research, financial modeling and Rabobank slide-production skills.

You are preparing a discussion for Apple's CFO and treasury team. The illustrative objective is to raise USD500m net to refinance upcoming maturities. Compare three-year and five-year USD bonds and discuss issuing now versus waiting. Present balanced conditional advice rather than a predetermined recommendation.

Ask me only material unanswered questions about the client objective, desired takeaway and bank positioning. Propose a concise storyline before drafting. For the bank proposition, use public Rabobank evidence and distinguish general capabilities from verified deal-specific credentials.

Retrieve relevant public filings/company financials and government yield history. Seek leverage, liquidity, maturity structure, interest coverage if supported, disclosed ratings and issue terms. Keep current issuer spreads, new-issue concession, rating affirmation and any missing inputs explicit. Treasury movements alone do not establish how Apple's all-in funding cost has changed.

Use dated source-linked evidence and explicit illustrative transaction assumptions. Run deterministic bond calculations, show net funding, price/yield/coupon conventions, fees and rate/spread sensitivities. Reconcile outputs and save calculation inputs and workings. Do not silently size notional to gross rather than net funding.

Deliver an editable .pptx using the Rabobank public-reference visual identity, plus an HTML/PDF preview if available, evidence.json, model.json or an inspection workbook, and a short gap/review log. Use conclusion-led headlines, meaningful charts and clear source notes. Include assumptions and next steps. Render and inspect every page before handing over. Do not send materials externally.

After I comment page by page, distinguish wording changes from model changes, recalculate affected outputs and create a new version.
