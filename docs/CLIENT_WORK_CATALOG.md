# Client-facing banking work and modelling modules

23 October 2026. Scope is financial analysis and client/IC/investor materials, excluding legal-document review, legal drafting and transaction execution. This catalog separates the full product from the demonstrated methods. Public vendor descriptions establish advertised workflows, not independently verified performance or private architecture.

[Model ML workflows](https://modelml.com/product/workflows) explicitly include take-private advisory, management presentations, CIM structuring, company profiles, target screens and IC materials. [Finster](https://www.finster.ai/product) describes cited research, background workflows, firm-format presentations and intent-shaped interactive output. [Rogo's product update](https://rogo.com/blog/may-product-update) describes reusable financial agents, MCP connections, versioned slide annotations and an artifact library. Established-bank practice spans advisory and financing; see [Goldman Sachs investment banking](https://www.goldmansachs.com/what-we-do/investment-banking) and [Rabobank financing capabilities](https://www.rabobank.com/products/finance-my-business). Our modular boundaries are an independent implementation choice, not a claim about those firms' code.

Single debt tranche; full integrated operating model excluded. |
| Growth / VC / private placement investment memorandum | Which funding round and return/dilution scenarios fit the business? | Cash runway, cohort/unit economics, cap table, ownership/dilution and scenario returns | Management operating data and financing preferences; public coverage is often insufficient | Future module with explicit information requests; no invented private-company data |
| Valuation / fairness-supporting discussion | Which valuation range is defensible, and what assumptions drive it? | DCF, trading/transaction comps, SOTP, equity bridge and sensitivity | Consistent estimates, prices, capital structure, comparable definitions and transaction terms | Dated broad industry references for context only; no fairness opinion or complete valuation conclusion |
| Investor / management presentation and recurring update | Explain performance, outlook, strategy and funding clearly | KPI/financial bridges, forecast changes and scenario comparisons | Approved disclosures, period reconciliations and management assumptions | Dynamic semantic frames, charts and sourced narrative; recurring monitoring and company-specific disclosure approval are future controls |

The architecture should serve these work products through reusable financial methods, rather than one unrelated agent per document type. A work product specifies the client questions, evidence requirements, methods, narrative structure, brand rules and review criteria. The model proposes a plan and composition; validated methods compute authoritative figures; the data pool retains observations and every output's dependencies. Human review covers both the argument and the underlying inputs. A generated presentation is a discussion draft, not evidence of suitability or a mandate.

## Public data routes and boundaries

| Source | Useful public content / delivery | Implemented route / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| [SEC EDGAR APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) | Company facts/submissions and XBRL filing observations | Fixed PEP/CPB/GIS normalized annual mapping, public fetch attempts and retained receipts; SEC may reject requests. A failed refresh never becomes a live-data claim. Wider company discovery/mapping requires additional reconciliation. |
| [U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center-data-chart-center/interest-rates) | Daily Treasury par-yield observations | Public curve fetch and dated version in the pool; par rates are not spot discount factors or executable issuer pricing. |
| [Bundesbank current Federal securities](https://www.bundesbank.de/en/statistics/overview-of-the-statistical-series/-/3-yields-of-current-federal-securities-914558) / [SDMX API](https://statistiken.bundesbank.de/statistiken-en/help-991452?article=bundesbank-sdmx-web-service-api-991448) | Official German government yield time series in CSV/SDMX | Live fixed-series 2/5/10-year EUR reference adapter and presentation frame; not used to price the USD examples. Currency and benchmark definitions must remain separate. |
| [Damodaran / NYU Stern sector table](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/datafile/vebitda.htm) | Public broad US industry EV/EBITDA references, including positive-EBITDA/all-firm definitions | Two relevant sectors retrieved into a dated source snapshot and the pool. Valuation date is not independently established from that table; these are not current target-specific trading or deal comps. Do not mechanically apply to the app's EBITDA proxy. |
| Public issuer annual/interim reports and investor presentations | Company narrative, tables and disclosures; different European reporting/hosting structures | A narrow Apple 2025 issuer-hosted annual-report HTML mapping feeds canonical facts. Other URLs remain registration only; general PDF/XHTML/table extraction, taxonomy mapping and human promotion are not implemented. |
| Public equity/index endpoints | Delayed/historical prices can support a demonstration if definitions and permitted use are understood | No live equity/index connector accepted yet. Public visibility does not establish redistribution or enterprise rights. Current stock-price inputs remain labelled assumptions. |

Rabobank's public sector and financing descriptions are retained separately from financial observations. They support a proposed client-relevance argument, not fabricated league-table rankings, confidential credentials, banker/team availability or a promise to underwrite. More persuasive pitches will require approved credentials, genuinely relevant comparable mandates and relationship knowledge, with proper provenance and rights.

## Minimum live-agent demonstration

The director supplies one natural-language assignment. Local Codex proposes the structured brief/storyline, then the host retrieves pinned observations through real MCP, calculates comparable actions in Rust and stores the financial work. A second bounded model stage composes headlines, narrative and typed frames from that financial context. Figures/chart data resolve to stored objects. Public source refresh is explicit and failed attempts remain visible. A follow-up instruction can propose validated scenario changes and regenerate materials; unresolved review remains visible and historical copies remain immutable.

This is a bounded planning/composition orchestrator, not unrestricted autonomous underwriting. Its current two model stages and supported methods should be evaluated on real paired banker tasks before scaling to the broader catalog. The five independent model gauntlets found product defects; they do not establish the requested 10× productivity or 5× client-impact multipliers.
