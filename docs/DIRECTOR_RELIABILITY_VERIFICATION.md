## Boundaries

The narrow Apple annual-report adapter does not establish general PDF/table extraction. SEC refusal is not reported as live success. German EUR references are not substituted into USD pricing. No current issuer spreads or equity-price feed has been established. The preliminary IPO/LBO models exclude full underwriting, forecast DCF and complex debt/covenant structures. The five earlier model gauntlets remain historical reviews of the prior correction baseline; this extension is covered by the executed checks above, not a newly claimed independent banker review. No human task observations, 10×/5× outcome, bank approval or production security acceptance is inferred.
