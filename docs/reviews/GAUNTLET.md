# Five independent banker-lens gauntlet passes

23 October 2026. The owner expressly requested five independent GPT-6.1 Sol reviewers, each thinking from the client-facing banker's perspective. Five fresh native review agents were run sequentially, with the original objective, prior findings, responses and unresolved objections included in each prompt. They are model reviewers simulating that lens, **not qualified human bankers**. The fifth agent later performed a bounded verification of its repairs and the owner's newly requested director-led flow; that follow-up is not a sixth independent round.

Raw reports remain intact, including unfavorable findings. Later repairs do not rewrite the original verdicts. A functional/evaluated score does not establish a human productivity ratio, banker acceptance, investment suitability or bank compliance.

The owner then expanded the scope to real conversational delegation, public ingestion, a data pool and a sourced bank/client storyline. Actual local Codex planning/composition, financial-context orchestration, retained public ingestion versions, the director-first interface and dynamic frame browsing were implemented. The fifth follow-up inspected that actual 12-page completed example, recorded SEC-refresh failure and successful Treasury ingestion, and correctly found two residual defects: brief-only turn changes could be discarded, and the bank/source frames overflowed. Those raw findings remain visible.

The final host persists all changed brief fields through the typed revision endpoint, rejects client/method/target substitution, refreshes bounded context and retains each successful follow-up instruction in conversation. An added fake-CLI regression reproduces the changed-criteria case and verifies target rejection. That suite is explicit contract testing, not proof of model inference. Dense-source and bank frames retain their complete content with responsive full-page layouts; final actual-page checks are recorded in [client verification](../client-verification.json). No hidden overflow, discarded qualification or invented human timing is treated as acceptable evidence.

## What was actually assessed

- Actual binary/API behavior in isolated databases: independent financial reconstruction, exact source/model persistence, controlled amendments, review gates, invalid/stale operations, immutable copies, bounded history, stable measurement pagination and document migration.
- Actual browser interactions: client/frame navigation, numerical evidence inspection, source-checking edit-buffer preservation, controlled late-response races and page fit at 1280×800.
- Actual local account inference, separately from fake fixtures: director assignment, public ingestion attempts, deterministic calculations and dynamic model-composed client frames. Source failures and unsupported methods remain explicit.
- Zero real human paired-task observations or independently verified client-use outcomes. The application starts with no invented baseline and makes both multipliers explicitly unproven.

## The business-value test still required

Use equivalent public-data tasks completed by real bankers with and without the tool, counterbalanced for learning and judged against the same acceptance criteria. Include research, modelling, materials, QA, senior review and rework in all-staff active minutes; record wall time separately. Retain failures. Baseline accepted-quality effort divided by assisted effort is the productivity ratio; backend latency and tokens are not that ratio.

Predefine distinct client questions or useful findings, and have a blinded qualified reviewer/client assess whether each was correctly resolved. Question coverage is a limited meeting-utility proxy; it is not realized client economics, mandate wins or a fivefold quality score. The [evaluation protocol](../CLIENT_WORKFLOW_EVALUATION.md) defines the denominators and limitations. The owner/qualified banker walkthrough remains open in GitHub #48. Five model critiques are useful defect discovery and cannot close that gate.
