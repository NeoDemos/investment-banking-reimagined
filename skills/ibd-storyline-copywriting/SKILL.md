---
name: ibd-storyline-copywriting
description: Develop or edit investment banking pitchbook storylines, executive summaries, slide headlines and investment memorandum copy for CFOs, boards and investors. Use for client-facing IBD writing, capital markets pitches, M&A positioning and page-by-page narrative revision.
---

Write as a banker helping a client make a decision. Establish the audience, decision, objective, alternatives and desired bank positioning from the brief. Ask for material missing direction; propose reasonable working assumptions for a rehearsal rather than creating a long questionnaire.

Develop an argument before drafting pages: client situation → decision criteria → alternatives → financial consequences → conditional recommendation → execution questions and next steps. Adapt this order to the mandate rather than forcing a fixed deck.

Use a conclusion-led headline on each page. Under it, state the evidence, explain why it matters to this client, and identify the relevant decision or action. Make headlines form a coherent argument when read alone. Prefer concrete language over adjectives such as leading, compelling or attractive. Do not use marketing copy to obscure weak evidence.

Separate reported facts, calculations, estimates, assumptions and advice. Retain qualifications in the client-visible copy. Describe stale evidence with its date. Do not turn an absence of data into a positive conclusion or a claim of certainty. Reconcile repeated figures to the same model/evidence record rather than typing variants into each page.

For debt pitches, distinguish government benchmarks from issuer spreads and executable issue terms. For buybacks, distinguish EPS effects from value creation. For M&A, explain strategic fit alongside valuation, funding, synergies, execution risks and downside. For an IPO or LBO, make valuation, capital structure and exit assumptions explicit.

Write the bank proposition around the client's needs and supported capabilities. Use supplied or public Rabobank evidence; never invent league-table positions, mandates, relationships, distribution strength or underwriting commitments. Public corporate positioning alone does not establish transaction-specific credentials.

Deliver a storyline table with page, proposed headline, client question, planned visual, evidence/model inputs and unresolved question. Then draft the requested materials. For slide-by-slide feedback, keep a revision log of headline changes, analytical changes and remaining questions. Recalculate numerical changes before revising conclusions.

Example editorial improvement:

- Weak: “Our comprehensive capital markets capabilities.”
- Better: “Agree the funding objective before choosing tenor and execution timing.”
- Weak: “Lower benchmark yields make issuance attractive.”
- Better: “Benchmark movements inform timing, while issuer spreads and funding needs determine the recommendation.”

Apply the companion rabo-banking-materials design workflow for Rabobank-style output; use the host's available PowerPoint capability for editable slides. Do not substitute advertising or social-media copywriting for investment banking judgment.
